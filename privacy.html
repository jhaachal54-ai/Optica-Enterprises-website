2. What Information We Collect

We collect information only when you voluntarily submit it through one of our website forms. This includes:

  • Contact form: name, phone number, email address, and message content.
  • Demo-request form: name, phone, email, organisation name, instrument of interest, and preferred timing.
  • Service-request form: name, phone, email, instrument details, and nature of the service issue.
  • Quick-quote bar: name and phone number.

We do not collect payment card numbers, government ID numbers, or any health or medical data through this website.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information you provide is used solely to:

  • Respond to your enquiry, quotation request, or service report.
  • Schedule product demonstrations or service visits at your request.
  • Send you follow-up information relevant to your enquiry (product catalogues, pricing, etc.).
  • Improve our services based on feedback you provide.

We do not use your information for automated marketing campaigns or unsolicited outreach beyond the scope of your original enquiry.

4. How Your Data Is Processed & Stored

Form submissions on this website are processed by Web3Forms (web3forms.com), a third-party form submission service. When you submit a form, your data is transmitted securely via HTTPS to Web3Forms, which delivers the submission to our email inbox. Web3Forms may retain submission logs for a limited period as per their own privacy policy, which you can review at web3forms.com/privacy.

Once received, your enquiry details are stored in our email system and used by our sales and service team. We do not maintain a separate CRM database that stores your personal data beyond our email correspondence.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

This website uses minimal browser-side storage:

  • Cookie consent flag (localStorage) — records whether you have acknowledged our cookie notice, so it is not shown on every page.
  • Colour scheme preference (localStorage) — stores your dark/light mode choice so it persists across visits.
  • Exit popup flag (sessionStorage) — records whether the exit-intent popup has been shown in the current session, so it appears only once.

None of these storage items contain personal information or are shared with third parties. We do not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party analytics on this website.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Your data is shared only with:

  • Web3Forms — as the technical intermediary for delivering your form submission to us (see Section 4).
  • Our service engineers — where necessary to fulfil a service visit or instrument installation you have requested.
  • Relevant brand / manufacturer representatives — only where you request a demo or quotation for a specific instrument and manufacturer involvement is required, and only with your implied consent through submitting such a request.

7. Data Retention

We retain your contact and enquiry information for as long as it is relevant to an active business relationship with you, or for a reasonable period thereafter in case of follow-up enquiries. If you have not had any contact with us for more than 3 years, we will delete your information from our records.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your personal data.
  • Object to us contacting you further.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at opticaenterprises70@gmail.com. We will respond within 7 business days.

9. Security

This website is served over HTTPS. All form data is transmitted using TLS encryption. We take reasonable technical precautions to protect information submitted through this website. However, no internet transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the security of data transmitted to us over the internet.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of this website after a change to this policy constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
