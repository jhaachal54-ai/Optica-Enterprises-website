Reagents & Consumables

Every instrument is calibrated, validated, and quality-tested specifically against the manufacturer's reagent formulations. Using third-party or non-approved reagents can produce inaccurate results, trigger false flags, accelerate wear on internal components, and void the instrument's warranty. Patient safety depends on using the correct reagents — we always supply genuine manufacturer products.
Shelf life varies by reagent type — most liquid reagents carry a 12–18 month shelf life from the manufacturing date. Reagents requiring refrigeration (2–8°C) are transported in insulated, cold-chain packaging and must be stored as directed on the product label. Dry cartridge-based reagents (such as those for the Mispa REVO and Mispa i3) are stored at room temperature and have a simpler storage profile. We will advise on correct storage for every reagent we supply.
There is generally no strict minimum order quantity for reagents. However, ordering in practical quantities reduces your cost per delivery and ensures you maintain a comfortable buffer stock. For regularly used reagents, we recommend keeping 2–4 weeks of stock on hand to cover any supply delays. Our team can help you plan reagent orders based on your monthly test volumes.
For clients in Delhi NCR, in-stock reagents are typically delivered within 1–3 business days of ordering. For other cities, delivery usually takes 3–7 business days depending on your location. We strongly advise ordering before your stock runs critically low to avoid any interruption to testing. For time-sensitive requirements, call or WhatsApp us directly for the fastest turnaround.
Yes. We supply the complete range of consumables for each instrument — including assay reagents, calibrators, multi-level quality control materials, wash solutions, sample cups, and any other accessories required for routine operation.
